# odin-recipes
top foundations classwork #z0001a - this is a  recipe webpage that host links to other internal pages which communicate recipes of  three  dishes which have been part of the native collection of Liberian food culture long before Liberia became the given name for this beautiful portion of African land.  

Don't pay too much mind to #z0001a, it is not too important. 
 
Without being too specific about the all the micro details, this webpage links to three old recipes native to Liberia. It is aimed at international  cooks and chefs 
who need culinary menu ideas outside of their everyday staples/variety to tryout in their kitchens.These are foods that few people eat everyday in Liberia.

Funny bet: There is no single day that passes by under the sun without a Liberian cooking and eating one of these dishes.
